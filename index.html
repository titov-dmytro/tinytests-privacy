Privacy Policy

TinyTests: Kids Math Quiz · last updated 26 November 2026

TinyTests (the "app", "we") is a maths practice app for children. This policy explains what the app stores and why. It is deliberately short.

Who is responsible

TinyTests is published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, or to have an account deleted, contact dmitry90titov@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details - the email address and display name a parent or guardian enters when creating an account. The name is used only to greet the learner inside the app.
  • Practice progress - XP, correct answers, the current level, per-level totals and the current streak, so progress survives a reinstall or a new device.
  • Reminder settings - whether the daily reminder is on and the time chosen for it.
  • Device messaging token - a technical identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, used only to deliver reminders.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or a child's real name or age.

How the information is used

Only to run the app: to sign the learner in, to save and restore progress, and to deliver the optional daily reminder.

Sharing

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which processes it on our behalf. There are no adverts, no advertising networks, no analytics brokers, and no sale or sharing of personal data with anyone else. Information is disclosed only if the law requires it.

Children's privacy

TinyTests is designed for children to use together with a parent or guardian, and the account is created by that adult. We ask only for what is needed to run the app, we serve no personalised advertising, and we do not build profiles of children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without consent, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

Traffic between the app and the servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The account password is never stored in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Progress is kept while the account exists. Email the address above to have the account and its progress deleted; it will be removed within 30 days. Turning the daily reminder off in Settings stops the reminder immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

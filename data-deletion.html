Delete your TinyTests data

TinyTests: Kids Math Quiz · com.tinytests.app

You can ask us to delete your TinyTests account and everything stored with it. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address you used to create the account, or include that address in the message so we can find the right account:

Request deletion by email

Or write to dmitry90titov@gmail.com with the subject "TinyTests data deletion request".

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (email address, display name) Deleted
Practice progress (XP, level, correct answers, streak) Deleted
Reminder setting and messaging token Deleted

We do not keep a copy of any of the above after the request is processed. We do not keep your data in a separate archive, and there is nothing to restore once it is gone.

How long it takes

Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request, and usually much sooner. You will get a confirmation email when it is done.

Before you ask us to delete it

Deleting the account cannot be undone, so:

  • You can stop the daily reminders at any time in the app under Settings → Daily reminder without deleting anything.
  • Signing out removes the account from the device but keeps the progress online, so you can sign back in later.
  • Deleting the app from the device does not delete the account on its own - please use the request above.
